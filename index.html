From a Kenner kitchen to two dining rooms

Richard Rose and Jackie Diaz started selling plates out of their home in Kenner. What began as word of mouth turned into a following — and by the end of 2021, the couple opened their first brick-and-mortar on St. Claude Avenue in the Upper Ninth Ward, painted in the yellow and green of the Jamaican flag.

The dining room stayed small and personal — reggae in the air, regulars trading banter over oxtails and curry chicken. Then a viral review from food critic Keith Lee sent the following well past the neighborhood, and Jamaican Jerk House opened a second location in Metairie, with décor inspired by Kingston itself.

Richard's jerk sauce — the one he's sold under his own name for years — is still the base of everything that comes out of the kitchen.
